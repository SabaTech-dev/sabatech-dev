Announcing SabaTech — Agentic QA Automation & Security for the AI Era

SabaTech launches as the European boutique combining human expertise with AI agents for quality, security, and trust in AI systems.

announcement ai-security qa-automation launch

The Problem

AI is transforming software development at unprecedented speed. Teams ship faster than ever — but security and quality often lag behind. Traditional QA and security tools weren’t built for autonomous agents, multi-agent architectures, or AI-generated code.

The result? Vulnerabilities reach production. Test coverage drops. Compliance becomes an afterthought.

Our Mission

At SabaTech, we believe AI systems deserve AI-powered security and quality assurance. Not as an afterthought, but built into every commit, every deployment, every workflow.

Our tagline says it all: Agentic QA Automation & Security for the AI Era.

What We Build

QA Framework

The first QA framework built for AI-assisted development. Automated E2E testing, quality gates, and agentic test generation — all from a single platform. Ship with confidence, every time.

Security Audit

AI security audits covering OWASP Agentic Applications 2026, EU AI Act compliance, NIST AI RMF evaluation, and specialized agent pentesting. Our AI agents scan every commit, every dependency, every container.

AI Orchestration

Design and implement orchestration architectures for multi-agent systems. Workflow management, coordination, and process automation with specialized agents.

  • 5 Specialized AI Agents — Coder, Security, QA Tester, DevOps, Research — working 24/7

No competitor offers this. A team of AI agents with human oversight, European data sovereignty, and compliance-first approach.

European Values
